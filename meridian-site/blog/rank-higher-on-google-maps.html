When someone nearby searches for what you do, the three businesses in Google's map results get most of the calls. Getting into that top three doesn't need a big ad budget. It comes from doing a handful of things properly, then keeping them up week after week.

How Google decides who shows up

Google says local results are based on three things:

  • Relevance is how well your profile matches what the person searched for.
  • Distance is how far your business is from the searcher, or from the location in their search.
  • Prominence is how well known and trusted your business is, including your reviews, your rating and your presence across the web.

You can't move your business closer to every customer, so the work goes into relevance and prominence.

1. Choose the right categories

Your primary category has a big influence on which searches you appear for. Pick the most specific one that describes your main service, such as "Emergency plumber" rather than "Contractor". Then add secondary categories for the other work you do.

2. Fill in everything

Complete every part of your profile: services with short descriptions, service areas, opening hours, attributes, your website and booking link, and a business description that naturally uses the words customers search for. Incomplete profiles look less trustworthy to customers and to Google.

3. Post every week

Google Business Profile lets you publish updates, offers and news. Regular posts show Google the business is active, and they give searchers a reason to choose you. Keep each post short, mention a specific service and suburb, and add a photo.

4. Add real photos regularly

Photos of your team, vehicles and finished jobs build trust far better than stock images. Add a few new ones every month rather than a big batch once a year.

5. Build a steady stream of reviews

Reviews are one of the clearest prominence signals, and they're what customers read before they call. Aim for a steady flow of new reviews rather than occasional bursts, and reply to every one. Our guide on how to get more Google reviews covers this step by step.

6. Make your website back it up

Your website should clearly list each service and the areas you cover, with your business name, address and phone number matching your Google profile exactly. A dedicated page for each main service helps Google connect your profile to those searches.

7. Keep your details consistent everywhere

Check that your business name, address and phone number are the same on directories such as Yellow Pages, True Local, Hipages and your social media profiles. Inconsistent details make it harder for Google to trust which information is correct.

8. Track your rankings properly

Searching your own business from your office only shows you one spot on the map. A ranking grid checks your position from many points across your service area, so you can see where you're strong and where you're invisible.
