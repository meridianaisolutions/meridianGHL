Last updated 24 October 2026

{{brand:legalName}} (ABN {{brand:abn}}) ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information we collect

  • From you: your name, business name, email, phone number and anything you tell us when you fill in a form, book a call, start a trial or contact us.
  • From our clients: when a business uses our service, it gives us the names, phone numbers and email addresses of its customers so we can send review requests on its behalf.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries, book calls and provide our services.
  • To send review requests and follow-ups on behalf of our clients.
  • To manage and optimise our clients' Google Business Profiles.
  • To bill clients and keep business records.
  • To improve our website and services, and to send you marketing if you've agreed to it.

Customers of our clients

If you received a review request from a business that uses us, we process your details as that business's service provider. The business is responsible for having a relationship with you and for giving us your details. Every message includes an easy way to opt out, and we honour opt-outs promptly, as required by the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM and messaging platform, email and SMS carriers, payment processors and Google (for Business Profile management). Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it.

Security

We store information with reputable cloud providers that use encryption in transit, and we limit access to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We'll respond within a reasonable time, usually 30 days.

Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us first and we'll try to resolve it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
