Google Business Profile FAQs

Why does my Google Business Profile matter so much?

When someone nearby needs your service, they search Google and the map results are the first thing they see. Google decides who appears there partly on how complete, active and well-reviewed each profile is.

How fast will I see results?

Your profile is optimised in the first week and posting starts straight away. Most profiles see the first real movement in rankings around months three to four, and your monthly ranking map shows the progress. Nobody can guarantee a position on Google, so we show you the numbers instead.

Do I have to approve every post?

It's up to you. Most owners let it run on its own. If you'd rather check things first, posts wait for your approval, and you can approve them straight from the email.

What happens to my profile if I cancel?

It stays yours. We remove ourselves as a manager and hand it back with every improvement in place.

Is there a contract?

No. There's no lock-in contract and no cancellation fee.

Is my Google account safe?

You add us as a manager through Google's own system, and you can remove us at any time. You always stay the owner.
