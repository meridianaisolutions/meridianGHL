Why reviews matter

Your competitors aren't better than you. They just have more reviews.

When someone searches "plumber near me", Google shows three businesses on the map. The ones with more recent, positive reviews win the click and the call. Most great local businesses simply forget to ask.

Reviews affect your ranking

Google says your review count and rating are part of how it decides local rankings.

Google tells you to ask

Google's own advice to businesses is to ask customers for reviews and to reply to them.

An active profile ranks better

Complete, up-to-date Business Profiles with fresh photos and posts are what Google calls "prominent".

Source: Google Business Profile Help, "Tips to improve your local ranking on Google".

FAQ

Questions business owners ask us

How many reviews will I get?

It depends on how many customers you've served and how happy they were. Past customer campaigns usually produce the biggest early jump, then new customers add reviews every week. On your setup call we'll look at your numbers and give you an honest estimate.

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Google encourages businesses to ask customers for reviews. We only ask real customers, we never offer incentives for reviews, we never write reviews, and we ask every customer the same way rather than only the happy ones. That keeps your profile safe.

How much work is it for me?

About 15 minutes on an onboarding call to connect your Google Business Profile and share your customer list. After that it runs on its own. Most clients just add new customers or connect the software they already use.

Will my customers find the messages annoying?

Requests are short, friendly and personalised, and follow-ups stop as soon as a review is left. Every message includes an easy way to opt out, as required by the Spam Act.

What if I get a bad review?

You'll be alerted straight away and we'll draft a calm, professional reply for you to approve. A few honest, well-handled reviews actually make your profile more believable.

Do I need to sign a long contract?

No. {{offer:contract}} We'd rather keep you because it works.

What else do you do besides reviews?

We also build websites and manage Google Business Profiles, or run all three as the {{brand:systemName}}. Our profile management optimises your Google Business Profile every week. It publishes keyword-targeted posts, adds photos and Q&A, keeps your services and details complete, answers reviews and tracks where you rank on the map across your service area.

Can you stop bad reviews from going on Google?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who says they can. Filtering out unhappy customers before they reach Google (called review gating) breaks Google's policies and can get a profile suspended. We ask every customer the same way, and we help you reply well to the occasional bad review.

What software do you connect to?

We can connect most job, booking and invoicing tools directly or through Zapier, so new customers are asked automatically. If your software isn't supported, you can add customers from your phone in seconds.

How often will my customers be asked?

One request after the job and up to two reminders, which you can adjust. Customers who use you regularly are only asked once every 30 days at most.

Do you work with businesses outside my city?

We work with service businesses right across Australia. Everything is done online, so location doesn't matter.
