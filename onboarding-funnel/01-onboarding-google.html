Connect your Google Business Profile

Your reviews show up on your Google Business Profile, so we'll connect it first. It takes about two minutes.

Add us as a manager

  1. Sign in to business.google.com with the Google account that owns your profile.
  2. Open your profile's menu and go to Business Profile settings, then Managers.
  3. Click Add, enter marcus@meridianaisolutions.com.au and pick Manager.
  4. Send the invite. We'll accept it on our side.

You stay the owner. Manager access lets us reply to reviews and post updates, and you can remove us any time.
